# Community code of conduct

We welcome thoughtful contributions regardless of experience or background. Focus discussions on designs, tests, evidence and reproducible behavior. Treat other participants respectfully, assume good intent while checking technical claims, and critique code rather than people.

Harassment, discriminatory abuse, personal attacks, doxxing, threats, and publication of someone else's private information are not acceptable. Maintainers may moderate or remove content, close discussions, or restrict participation to keep the project constructive.

For security problems, use the private reporting process in `SECURITY.md`, not public issues. For conduct reports, contact a repository maintainer privately through the account's available contact mechanism; until a dedicated moderation address is published, do not send private or sensitive reports in public discussion threads.
